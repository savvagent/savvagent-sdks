Support React 19 and Next.js 16. The peer ranges are widened to `react >=16.8.0 <20` for `@savvagent/react`, and to `next >=13.0.0 <17` and `react >=18.0.0 <20` for `@savvagent/nextjs`. Lower bounds are unchanged. Both packages are now built and tested against React 19.3 (and Next 16.4 for `@savvagent/nextjs`).
